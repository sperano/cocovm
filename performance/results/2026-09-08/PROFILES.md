# Sampling profile summaries

Two separate release-build captures used `/usr/bin/sample <pid> 2 1 -file profile.txt`: a requested 2-second capture at 1 ms intervals, started after the 3-second warmup. Both ran on ARM64 macOS 26.6.2 (25G83). The TV capture began at 2026-09-08 00:35:01.335 PDT; the headless DAC capture began at 00:35:15.800 PDT. The neighboring instrumented results are retained in [profile-tv.json](profile-tv.json) and [profile-core.json](profile-core.json). The raw sampling reports are summarized here; regenerate them with [the capture commands](RUNS.md). Profiler parameters are defined in `scripts/perf/baseline.py`.

The TV capture contains 1,119 main-thread observations. The leading application leaf is `cocovm::display::process`, with 115 observations (10.3% of main-thread observations). The stack is `CocoApp::window_ui → step_emulation → upload_framebuffer_texture → display::process`; another 14 observations reach `ColorImage::from_rgba_unmultiplied`. Source pointers: `crates/coco-egui/src/app/frame.rs:164` and `crates/coco-egui/src/display.rs:293`. These are observable CPU work in display processing and image conversion. The capture does not resolve the individual inlined TV-effect passes well enough to rank them.

TV presentation also occupies substantial main-thread stack time: one immediate-viewport flush subtree has 282 observations, including 215 in `NSCGLSurface synchronize → NSWaitUntilHostTime → mach_msg2_trap`. Context switching and surface attachment also appear. These are inclusive wall-sampling counts containing waits and IPC; they do not establish 282 observations of CPU computation or GPU execution. The upload stack contains 22 observations in `Painter::set_texture → glTexImage2D`, including 20 in the Apple driver’s texture-layout conversion routine. This establishes CPU-side backend texture work, not measured GPU transfer bandwidth.

Across all TV threads, the leaf summary is dominated by semaphore waits, work-queue waits, and Mach messaging. The audio IO thread has 1,117 of 1,119 observations in `semaphore_wait_signal_trap`. Do not rank these aggregated blocked-thread counts as CPU hotspots or infer an audio deadlock. The neighboring 10-second instrumented run recorded 597 emulated fields, 876 display conversions and texture enqueues, and 1,076,428,800 requested texture bytes. It also recorded 51 missing audio frames and 7,543 overflow frames; these belong to the profiler-perturbed run and must remain separate from the clean baseline matrix.

The headless DAC capture contains 1,530 observations on its single sampled main thread, with execution stacks rather than a dominant blocking wait. Its leading leaf counts are `gime_video::paint_scanline` 565 (36.9%), `SystemBus::read` 237 (15.5%), `MC6809::step` 132 (8.6%), and `Machine::step_instruction` 109 (7.1%). These are separate leaf counts, not inclusive totals. The DAC scenario therefore exercises rendering and CPU/bus execution as well as audio; it is not an isolated audio benchmark. Source pointers: `crates/coco-core/src/gime_video.rs`, `crates/coco-core/src/bus.rs`, `crates/mc6809/src/exec.rs`, and `crates/coco-core/src/machine/run.rs`.

The DAC stack directly exposes audio-event allocation churn: `MC6809::step → SystemBus::note_audio_write → RawVec<AudioEvent>::grow_one → finish_grow → allocation/reallocation`. One `note_audio_write` subtree has 79 observations, including 77 in `grow_one`; other observations land in allocator frees below `end_of_line`. In `crates/coco-core/src/machine/audio.rs`, `flush_line_audio()` takes ownership of the event vector with `std::mem::take` and drops it after each line; `crates/coco-core/src/bus/audio_bridge.rs` pushes new events into the replacement vector. This code supplies a concrete explanation consistent with the sampled grow/free stacks. The neighboring measured run recorded 47,104 fields, 24,682,496 allocation/reallocation requests, and 3,554,279,424 requested bytes: exactly 524 requests per field. Allocation counters measure traffic, not retained memory; the sample report's physical footprint was 1,872 KiB.

These are single short, instrumented captures, useful for choosing follow-up measurements rather than proving optimization gains. Actual observations are fewer than the nominal 2,000 implied by a perfect 1 ms interval. Optimized Rust symbols are mangled and many functions are inlined; the reports contain function names and offsets, not source-line attribution. Inclusive stack counts overlap and must not be summed. No GPU timing, input-to-photon latency, or hardware-counter profile was captured. Keep the profiler runs out of clean throughput comparisons.
